Projects

01

GAMELAB UBISOFT 2026 GAMEPLAY & SYSTEMS / AUDIO LEAD

Arcadium Trip

A fast-paced 4-player-versus shooting platformer built for the Ubisoft Game Lab competition high "arcade" polish under strong technical rigor.

  • Player controller & combat stomps, projectiles, torpedoes, custom aim assist for fluid arena fights.
  • Systems architecture core loop via a custom state machine (RoundManager) and a dynamic respawn algorithm that prevents unfair deaths.
  • Game feel & UI DOTween scoreboard reveals, persistent FadeManager for seamless transitions.
  • Audio robust AudioManager with dynamic crossfades + the full 8-bit OST.
  • Delivery technical pillar during final crunch: merge-conflict firefighting, codebase stabilization.
  • Unity
  • C#
  • 4P Versus
  • DOTween
  • OST

GAME JAM DESIGN / GAMEPLAY / SOUND

Re-Orbit

A gravity-shifting platformer born from a game jam the cycle restarts endlessly, unless you break the loop and reunite with the other orbs.

  • Gravity shift switch polarity and rotate gravity by 90° or 180° without ever trapping the player.
  • Hidden alternate routes discovery feels intentional, never accidental.
  • Narrative-mechanic harmony story, gameplay and jam theme stay tightly intertwined, down to two endings.
  • Unity
  • Level design
  • Alt endings
  • OST

WINTER 2025 FACULTY EVENT VR GAMEPLAY LOGIC

Project DARTS VR

A room-scale VR saloon where players score for their faction two ways: hit the dartboard, or hit the jackpot on a slot machine feeding a live scoreboard.

  • Darts scoring compute the polar angle of impact, map angle/ring to wedge and multiplier.
  • Slot machine reel rotation, timing/stop resolution and payline matching.
  • Unity
  • VR
  • Room-scale

PERSONAL PROTOTYPE PHYSICS

Ragdoll Combat

A physics-based prototype exploring dynamic ragdoll behavior and smooth transitions between animated and physics-driven states.

  • Rigidbody ragdoll fine-tuned joint limits across the full skeleton.
  • Animation ⇄ physics blending natural reactions without snapping.
